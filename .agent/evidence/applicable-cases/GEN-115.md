## What this establishes

Fault injection IS executed, which this row denied. The drill injects two fault classes repeatedly against a live vault -- TOTAL LOSS (the vault file and its WAL siblings deleted) and CORRUPTION (the live vault overwritten in place) -- then recovers through the PRODUCTION restore command and reconciles the recovered state by content digest through an independent connection, with RPO/RTO measured against an enforced threshold. The exact-artifact lane adds a hard process kill with the ledger read back in a new process, and the stress trial injects backup destruction under concurrent load.

## What this does not establish

The faults are filesystem-level on one host: no disk-error injection, no power-loss or kernel-level fault, and no network partition (the product serves no network). The drill covers the vault, not the whole machine.
