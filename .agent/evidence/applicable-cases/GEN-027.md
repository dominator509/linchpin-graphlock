Mutation-based testing is executed at two levels, and this row used to deny both. ## What this does not establish

Neither mechanism is a general-purpose mutation-testing platform: coverage is the 13 declared mutations and ten fuzz targets, not the whole codebase, and no mutation-score threshold is enforced. Two earlier mutations that SURVIVED were withdrawn rather than claimed as caught.
