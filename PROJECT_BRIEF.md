# Project LINCHPIN - Product Brief

## Problem
Independent inventors can generate ideas but usually lack an integrated, evidence-first system for determining whether an idea occupies meaningful technical/market whitespace, preserving human conception, stress-testing patent claims, producing a professionally structured filing package, managing disclosure/deadline risk, and turning protected IP into a licensing, sale, or fundraising campaign. Existing products concentrate on patent-professional drafting/prosecution or search; generic AI tools lack durable provenance and routinely blur research, legal conclusions, and commercialization claims.

## Product
LINCHPIN is a local-first Windows desktop Patent Intelligence and Commercialization OS. It combines an Opportunity Radar, Human Conception Lab, Prior Art War Room, LINCHPIN defensibility engine, Design-Around Tournament, Claim Architect, Specification/Figure Studio, Filing Concierge, Docket, Prosecution Workbench, Portfolio/Continuation Architect, preliminary FTO landscape, and Commercialization Studio. It is an evidence system before it is an AI chat system.

## Users
Primary: independent inventors, first-time filers, technical founders, engineers, and small R&D teams. Secondary: patent practitioners, accelerators, universities, consultants, and licensing/commercialization teams who choose to consume its work product.

## Core live-fire outcomes
UO-01 encrypted invention workspace + Human Conception Ledger.
UO-02 evidence-backed blue-ocean opportunity discovery/ranking.
UO-03 reproducible prior-art kill-search + LINCHPIN report.
UO-04 claim/spec/embodiment/figure architecture + support lint.
UO-05 versioned provisional/nonprovisional draft package + guided human Patent Center handoff.
UO-06 docket, patent-pending/priority/disclosure events, receipts and deadlines.
UO-07 Office Action ingestion, rejection mapping, response/amendment workspace.
UO-08 commercialization package, targets, valuation scenarios and disclosure-safe outreach assets.
UO-09 provider transport switching with equivalent schemas/provenance.
UO-10 MCP client/server interoperability with policy enforcement.
UO-11 crash -> sanitized Repair Capsule -> approved coding-agent/Git repair path.
UO-12 interruption/restart persistence with no invented state.

## Success metrics
- 100% of material research claims cite locally stored evidence snapshots or structured official-source records.
- 100% of claim limitations map to specification/figure support or are flagged before export.
- 0 silent public disclosures, legal filings, payments, outreach sends, or Git writes.
- 0 provider subscription auth tokens copied into LINCHPIN storage.
- 100% of release requirements map requirement -> implementation -> test -> evidence -> artifact digest.
- All applicable GraphLock release gates pass; every one of 484 capabilities is accounted.

## Out of scope for v1
Autonomous legal representation; guaranteed patentability/FTO/validity/enforceability; unattended filing/signing/payment; native mobile; multi-tenant cloud SaaS; global patent-corpus mirroring; CAPTCHA/paywall bypass; marketing that exposes Confidential material before a filing/override gate.
